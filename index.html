AI

SoilSense AI — Intelligent Soil Analysis

A Civil Engineering research platform for image-assisted soil classification. Samples were collected across Mysuru, Mandya, Bidar, Raichur, Kerala and Mangaluru. Laboratory investigations include specific gravity, sieve analysis, liquid limit and mini compaction tests. The initial web platform is deployed; dataset preparation, model training and validation are ongoing development stages.
